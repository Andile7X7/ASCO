About ASIHLANGANENI CIVIC MOVEMENT

A civic movement dedicated to unity, accountability, and the restoration of formal governance within our communities.

Who We Are

ASIHLANGANENI CIVIC MOVEMENT (ASCO) is a community-centric civic organization established to address the critical gaps in service delivery and local governance. We operate as a non-partisan movement that brings together residents to demand transparency and foster development.

Constitutional Foundation

Our mandate is derived directly from the legal framework of the Republic of South Africa, ensuring all actions are grounded in constitutional order.

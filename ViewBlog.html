Empowering Local Communities through Ward-Based Budgeting

How transparent financial allocation at the ward level is transforming service delivery and accountability.

person

Governance Research Committee

May 14, 2024

The implementation of ward-based budgeting stands as a cornerstone of ASCO's strategic vision for localized governance. By decentralizing fiscal authority, we ensure that resources are not just allocated, but are directed toward the specific, self-identified needs of each community. This approach fosters a direct link between municipal revenue and visible, community-led development projects.

“Decentralization is not just a policy shift; it is the fundamental restoration of dignity to our local wards.”

Transparency in this process is non-negotiable. Every cent tracked, every project audited, and every decision made in consultation with ward committees ensures that the movement remains accountable to the people it serves. This is not merely a policy; it is a restoration of the constitutional mandate for civic oversight and participation.

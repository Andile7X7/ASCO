About ASCO

Restoring Civic Dignity

ASIHLANGANENI CIVIC MOVEMENT (ASCO) is a principled organization established to bridge the gap between governance and the grassroots community. We focus on localized development, addressing service delivery failures, and tackling the scourge of unemployment.

Our approach is rooted in the belief that community participation is the cornerstone of sustainable development. By fostering a culture of accountability, we ensure that resources are utilized effectively for the benefit of all citizens.
